## Summary

- **15 products at or below ₹2,500:** 5% GST.
- **15 products above ₹2,500:** 18% GST.
- **Average net revenue:** ₹2,907.49 per product (simple average, not sales-weighted).
- **Average gross margin:** ₹1,495.83 per product, or 51.45%.
- **The margin is a product gross margin, not net profit.** Reverse logistics, payment fees, marketing, fulfilment and fixed costs come out of it.
