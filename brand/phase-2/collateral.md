## 9. File naming and handoff

- `santvana_[asset]_[size]_[colour]_v01.pdf` for print; `santvana_[asset]_[size]_[colour]_v01.svg` for vector.
- Keep the outlined logo SVG and the source generator. Do not flatten the logo into a PNG for print.
- Include the 6 mm measurement on the proof, not in a separate email.
- Every print file gets a `CONCEPT — NOT FOR PRINT` slug until the attorney opinion, native-speaker review and 6 mm proof are signed off.
- No file leaves the folder without the legal panel and the claims check.

---

## 10. What is deliberately missing

- **No business cards with a domain** until the domain is owned.
- **No QR codes** until the destination is live and the domain is owned.
- **No price list** until the Phase 3 catalogue and GST/HSN data exist.
- **No press kit** until the founder story facts and the name clearance are resolved.
- **No retail signage** in v1.
- **No "sustainable", "vegan" or "recycled" claim** on any collateral until the supplier certificate exists.
