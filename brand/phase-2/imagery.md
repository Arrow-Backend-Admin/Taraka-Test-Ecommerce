# Imagery — Sāntvana

**Phase 2 · 30 October 2026 · concept only**
No print, domain or ad spend until the attorney opinion in `../naming.md` §6 is back. No image on this page is a final asset.

---

## 1. The idea

**The ground is the hero.** Sāntvana is footwear for the walk between the station and the desk, the classroom and the bus stop, the site and the evening. The photography should look like India on an ordinary Tuesday: overcast light, concrete, tile, asphalt, paver blocks, wet edges, dust in the seams. The shoe is worn, not floating. The person is mid-day, not mid-yoga.

**No wellness cues.** No lotus, no hands in prayer, no candles, no incense, no pastel backdrops, no spa stones, no "mindful" bare feet on linen. The name means solace, but the visual language is relief you can walk on — not a retreat.

**No fake claims.** Do not photograph a shoe on a wet tile and caption it "slip-proof" unless the test protocol and coefficient-of-friction result exist. Do not show a recycled-material story until the supplier certificate exists. The image can show wet ground; the caption cannot claim a test result.

## 2. Art direction

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Natural, overcast, early morning or late afternoon. Hard sun is allowed only as a deliberate shadow study. No softbox glow, no golden-hour haze. |
| **Colour grade** | Neutral warm. Lifted blacks, muted greens, clay as the only saturated accent. No teal-and-orange, no faded film preset, no heavy grain. |
| **Ground** | Always visible. Concrete, tile, asphalt, paver, stairwell, platform, workshop floor, wet pavement. The ground is a character, not a backdrop. |
| **Camera** | 45° three-quarter, low angle, and true side profile. One macro of the sole tread. One top-down pair. No fisheye, no drone, no floating product. |
| **Crop** | 4:5 and 1:1 for product and social; 9:16 for stories; 16:9 only for email headers. Keep the shoe and the ground in the same frame. |
| **People** | Real feet, real ages, real urban India. 22–34. Wide feet, narrow feet, darker and lighter skin. No fitness models, no celebrity, no "perfect" pedicure. Faces are optional; hands only when tying a lace. |
| **Wear** | Show scuffs, creases, dust and a slightly flattened sole. A pristine shoe on a clean white sweep is a catalogue record, not the hero image. |
| **Retouching** | Keep skin texture, dust and scuffs. Remove only distractions: a stray cable, a logo on a background sign, a bin. Do not smooth, slim or lengthen feet. Do not composite a shoe into a location it was not shot in. |
| **Rights** | Model release for every identifiable person. Location permit for private property. No recognisable third-party brand marks. No image of a minor without guardian consent. |

## 3. Shot list for a drop

Twelve frames per drop, shot in one day in one city. The list is deliberately small; two drops a year, not twelve.

1. **Hero pair, 45°** — both shoes, on the ground, natural light, 4:5.
2. **Sole tread macro** — the three-bar tread geometry, raking light, 1:1.
3. **On-foot walking, 3/4** — mid-stride, ground visible, 4:5.
4. **Wet pavement step** — one foot on wet concrete or tile, no grip claim in the caption, 4:5.
5. **Stair descent** — heel counter and flex, low angle, 4:5.
6. **Transit platform** — waiting, weight on one foot, 16:9 for email.
7. **Seated lace-tying** — hands only, no face required, 1:1.
8. **Heel close-up** — heel counter, collar and sock line, 1:1.
9. **Toe-box flex** — forefoot bend, side profile, 1:1.
10. **Side profile** — the whole shoe, clean ground, 4:5.
11. **Top-down pair** — laces, toe shape, 1:1.
12. **Unboxing** — box, tissue, care card, shoes, 4:5.

**Every frame needs:** the ground, the shoe, and one true detail (a seam, a scuff, a wet edge, a tread lug). If the frame could be any brand's shoe, it is not a Sāntvana frame.

## 4. Do / do not

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Show the shoe on the ground it is built for | Float the shoe on a pastel sweep |
| Use overcast, ordinary light | Use a spa, a candle, a lotus or a linen altar |
| Show real wear and real feet | Smooth the skin, slim the foot, whiten the sole |
| Crop at the ankle and the sole | Crop at the face and lose the shoe |
| Keep the palette warm and neutral | Grade teal-and-orange or crush the blacks |
| Use one clear subject per frame | Stack three shoes, two props and a plant |
| Caption only what is evidenced | Caption "slip-proof", "vegan", "recycled" or "sustainable" without a certificate |
| Use a flat scrim for text on image | Use a gradient scrim or a soft glow |
| Show a wide toe box if the last has one | Claim "Indian feet" without a documented last process |

---

## 5. Image + type

- Text on image sits on a **flat ink or paper scrim**, never a gradient. The scrim must get the text/background pair to 4.5:1.
- The logo on image uses the reverse SVG with a flat scrim. Do not place the mark on a busy area without one.
- Keep the lower third of a 4:5 frame clear for the caption and the mark. Do not put the logo over the shoe.
- The **Long Line** rule can sit above or below the image, never across the shoe.
- Social captions use the same voice as the site: plain, specific, no exclamation marks. The image does not need a caption that repeats the tagline.

## 6. Image library rules

- File names: `drop-01_urban-runner_hero-45_4x5_v01.jpg`. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces.
- Store originals in Cloudflare R2 under `brand/drop-01/`; keep the camera original and a web derivative. Do not overwrite the original.
- Alt text: describe the shoe, the ground and the action. Do not stuff the brand name. Example: "A grey running shoe mid-stride on wet concrete, shot from a low three-quarter angle."
- No image goes live until the model/location release and the claims check are recorded.
- The 6 mm print proof is separate from the image proof. A beautiful photo does not prove the logo size.

---

## 7. What we are not shooting

- No "founder holding a shoe" portrait until the founder supplies the real story and approves the frame.
- No factory floor until the supplier relationship and worker consent are confirmed.
- No "made in India" hero until the manufacturing location and legal label are confirmed.
- No charity, orphanage or NIMHANS association in imagery. Those are founder-supplied story facts, not verified partnerships. Do not imply a donation or endorsement.
- No before/after foot transformation. That is a medical claim.
