**Phase 2 · 30 October 2026 · concept only — not for print**
No print, domain or ad spend until the attorney opinion in `../naming.md` §6 is back. The 6 mm measured wordmark proof is pending. No packaging file is a print release.

---

## 1. The idea

The box is a **quiet object**: paper, ink, one clay rule, one true sentence. It should feel like something you keep for storage, not something you tear through. No foil, no laminate, no soft-touch, no plastic sleeve, no fake wax seal, no "unboxing moment" theatre.

The unboxing sequence is simple:

1. **Sleeve or lid** — symbol on the front, nothing else.
2. **Tissue** — tread field at 8–12% tint; shoes wrapped, not bagged.
3. **Care card** — the promise and the care instruction, no marketing.
4. **Shoes** — one pair, one size, one fit note.
5. **Sticker** — the only place the tagline appears inside the box.

### Front

- **Stacked lockup**, 72 mm wide, centred, ink on paper.
- Clear space: 1× Latin cap height, minimum 12 mm on the box.
- No tagline on the front. The mark is enough.
- Optional: a 4 mm clay rule under the lockup, 40 mm wide, centred.

### Side

- **Horizontal lockup**, 95 mm wide, left-aligned, 16 mm from the edge.
- Under it: **“Made for the long way home.”** in Archivo 500, 9 pt.
- Under that: **“Free size exchange twice · 30-day returns from delivery.”** in Inter 400, 7.5 pt.
- Bottom: size, colour, style name, batch code.

### Back

- Fit note: “True to size. Wide feet: take a half size up.” — only if the Phase 3 fit data supports it.
- Materials table — only with confirmed supplier data.
- Care: “Clean with a soft brush and mild soap. Air-dry away from direct heat.”
- Returns and exchange summary, with the policy URL placeholder **not printed** until the domain is owned.
- QR code to the size guide — only after the domain is live.
- Legal panel: legal entity, address, MRP inclusive of all taxes, GST/HSN, country of origin, batch.

### Base

- Barcode, MRP, size, colour, batch, country of origin.
- No logo. The base is for the scanner and the law, not the brand.

## 9. Legal and compliance panel

Every box and mailer needs the following before print. These are not design choices.

- Legal entity name and registered address.
- Manufacturing location and country of origin.
- MRP in INR, inclusive of all taxes.
- GST/HSN code.
- Batch number and date of manufacture.
- Importer name and address, if applicable.
- Customer care contact and grievance officer, as required.
- Return and exchange policy summary.

**No legal panel, no print.** The Phase 3 master data supplies the values; do not use placeholder text on a physical box.

## 10. Print production notes

- **No print release until:** attorney opinion, native-speaker review, 6 mm measured wordmark proof, final legal panel, supplier certificates for any material claim.
- **Colour proof:** drawdown on the actual uncoated board. Ink on uncoated board will look darker and warmer than on screen.
- **Logo size proof:** measure the actual Devanagari wordmark height on the printed board. It must be **≥ 6 mm**. Do not measure the point size.
- **Barcode:** verify the EAN-13 scan at 80% and 100% on the actual stock.
- **Dieline:** a printer-supplied dieline is required; this document is a specification, not a dieline.
- **No plastic:** no polybag, no plastic window, no plastic tape. Paper tape only. If the printer cannot do paper tape, flag it before sampling.
- **No sustainability claim:** do not print “recycled”, “sustainable”, “eco” or “plastic-free” until the supplier certificate and the legal review exist. The absence of plastic is a spec, not a marketing claim.
