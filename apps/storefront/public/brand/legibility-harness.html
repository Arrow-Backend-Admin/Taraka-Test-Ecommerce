Phase 2 · legibility harness · v2

Test the mark, not the headline.

This harness renders the selected anusvara form at CSS sizes 16, 20, 24, 28 and 32 px, at device-pixel ratios 1×, 1.5× and 2×, against 40/255 and 128/255 alpha thresholds. It measures the anusvara dot and the full-word त्व counter. Change the font name to the final Devanagari face and run it again.

Ready.

1. Anusvara dot

Dot = topmost separate component above the shirorekha in सांत्वना. Solid-pixel size is the bounding box at the stated alpha threshold; area is in device pixels. The 20 px floor is CSS px, so a high-DPR screen gives more device pixels but does not lower the CSS floor.

2. त्व conjunct

Measured in the full word सांत्वना, cropped to the त्व region. Two checks: (a) the enclosed counter must survive at the threshold; (b) the horizontal ink-density waist must be ≤ 50% of the lower adjacent peak. The counter is the stricter check on 1×. The isolated conjunct is only a unit check; the full word governs.

1× size sweep at 50% alpha

The first passing full-word त्व counter size on 1× establishes the practical 1× minimum. The 20 px floor remains the absolute floor; it is not the safe 1× size.

3. Specimens

The same canvas at 1×, 1.5× and 2× device pixels. The CSS size is identical; only the device-pixel density changes. At 20 and 24 px on 1×, look for the त्व counter closing.

4. Zoomed 1× proof

Full word at 20, 24, 28 and 32 px on a 1× display, cropped to the त्व region and enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. This is the pixel evidence behind the sweep: the counter closes at 20 and 24 px, and opens at 28 px.

Rule: below 20 px, use the Latin monogram or symbol, never the Devanagari wordmark. The absolute floor is 20 px on screen and 6 mm measured wordmark height in print. If the 1× solid threshold closes the त्व counter at 20 or 24 px, use 28 px on 1× or the Latin monogram. 20 px is approved only at DPR ≥ 1.5.
Final-face re-run: Noto Sans Devanagari 600 (Google Fonts test build; the /brand route self-hosts the same family). Print floor is not proven by this screen test; approve a physical 6 mm measured wordmark height proof on final stock.
